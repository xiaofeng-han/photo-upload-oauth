photo-upload

A personal tool that backs up one person's self-hosted Immich photo and video library to Google Photos.

Personal use only. photo-upload is written and operated by Xiaofeng Han for his own Google account. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and it has no other users.

What it does

  • Uploads original photos and videos (converting unsupported formats locally while preserving EXIF timestamps and GPS metadata) from the owner's read-only Immich storage to the owner's Google Photos library.
  • Runs exclusively on the owner's private home server and Mac. There is no hosted cloud service.
  • Displays live upload progress, errors, and bandwidth controls in a local macOS menu bar app.

Access it requests

  • Google Photos Library API: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/photoslibrary.appendonly, used solely to upload media items into the owner's Google Photos timeline without reading or modifying existing cloud items.

How data is handled is described in the privacy policy. Use is governed by the terms of service.
